## Future Directions for Intelligent Robotics and Automation

Future Directions for Intelligent Robotics and Automation

**As AI, actuation, and perception technologies converge, we can expect to see humanoid platforms become increasingly viable and commercial.** The next wave of breakthroughs will be driven by advancements in world-model-driven simulation, multi-agent orchestration, and specialized physical AI. These developments will enable robots to perceive, reason, and act with limited oversight, making them more autonomous and effective.

One key area of focus will be multi-agent orchestration, which will enable teams of heterogeneous agents to collaborate in complex environments such as warehouses, farms, and factories. This will be achieved through the use of learning-based planners, graph-based policies, and other coordination methods.

Some potential future applications of intelligent robotics and automation include:

• **Biomedical and environmental applications**: Miniaturization and microrobotics will enable the development of small robots capable of sensing and limited actuation, opening up new possibilities for medical diagnosis and treatment, as well as environmental monitoring and remediation.
• **Autonomous mobility**: Multi-agent orchestration will enable the development of autonomous vehicles that can navigate complex environments and interact with other agents, such as pedestrians and other vehicles.
• **Industrial automation**: Specialized physical AI will enable the development of robots that can perform complex tasks in industrial settings, such as assembly and inspection.
