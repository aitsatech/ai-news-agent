## Introduction to Global Robotics Trends

**The robotics industry is witnessing a significant shift towards increased automation, artificial intelligence, and innovation, transforming the way robots interact with humans and their environment.**

As we enter 2026, the International Federation of Robotics has identified the top 5 global robotics trends that will shape the industry in the coming year. These trends are driven by advancements in technology, changing consumer needs, and the increasing demand for efficient and productive solutions. Some of the key trends include:

• **AI & Autonomy in Robotics**: Robots that use artificial intelligence and machine learning to perform tasks independently, making them more efficient and productive.
• **Robots: IT Meets OT**: The integration of information technology (IT) and operational technology (OT) to create more connected and intelligent robots.
• **Generative AI**: The use of generative AI to create new designs, models, and solutions for robotics applications.
• **Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS)**: The shift towards a subscription-based model for robotics, allowing users to access robots and their capabilities without the need for upfront investment.
• **Human-Robot Collaboration (HRC)**: The increasing focus on developing robots that can safely and effectively work alongside humans, enhancing productivity and efficiency.

## Emerging Technologies in Robotics

**Emerging Technologies in Robotics**

The robotics industry is witnessing a significant transformation with the integration of cutting-edge technologies. **Artificial intelligence (AI) and autonomy are expected to be the top trends in robotics for 2026, according to the International Federation of Robotics (IFR).** This shift is driven by the increasing demand for robots that can perform complex tasks independently and make decisions without human intervention.

Generative AI is also gaining traction in robotics, enabling robots to learn from data and adapt to new situations. The IFR has identified three other key trends in robotics for 2026: robots that integrate information technology (IT) and operational technology (OT), and the growth of collaborative robots (cobots).

Some of the key applications of emerging technologies in robotics include:
* **Robotics datasets:** Egocentric, teleoperation, simulation, and multimodal robotics datasets are being developed to enable robots to learn from data and improve their performance.
* **Robot autonomy:** AI-powered robots are being designed to perform tasks independently, without human intervention.
* **Robot-human collaboration:** Cobots are being developed to work alongside humans, improving productivity and safety in various industries.
