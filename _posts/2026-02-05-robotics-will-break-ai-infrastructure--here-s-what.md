## Rethinking AI Systems for a Robotics-Driven Future

**The AI Revolution is About to Get Physical**

As we hurtle towards a future dominated by robotics, it's clear that artificial intelligence (AI) systems are undergoing a radical transformation. Gone are the days of clunky, human-controlled workflows. Instead, we're witnessing the dawn of physical AI, where agentic and multi-agent systems take center stage. These AI systems will manage entire workflows, while humanoid and physical robots advance from demonstrations to targeted pilots in factories, warehouses, and labs.

Key drivers of this revolution include:

• **Predictive math**: AI systems that can predict and adapt to changing environments, making them more resilient and efficient.
• **Cooperative robots**: Robots that work together seamlessly, leveraging each other's strengths to achieve complex tasks.
• **Task-specific AI**: AI systems tailored to specific tasks, leading to increased productivity and reduced errors.
• **New data economy**: A shift towards a data-driven economy, where AI systems are the primary drivers of innovation and growth.
