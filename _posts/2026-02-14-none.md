7. **Cloud Infrastructure**: To ensure scalability, reliability, and high availability, we will deploy the system on a cloud infrastructure like Amazon Web Services (AWS) or Google Cloud Platform (GCP). This will enable seamless deployment, scaling, and management of the system.
